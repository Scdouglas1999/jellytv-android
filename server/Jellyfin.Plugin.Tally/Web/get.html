TALLY

Get Tally on your TV

Live games with scores, a switcher and multiview. About three minutes, once, mostly from this phone. After that the app keeps itself up to date.

What kind of TV is it?

  1. Send the free Downloader app to your TV from this phone. It is only the installer.

    Send to my Google TVChromecast, Shield, onn, Sony, TCL, Hisense Send to my Fire TVFire Stick, Fire TV Cube, Fire TV sets

    Google: tap the small arrow beside Install (or "Install on more devices"), tick your TV, tap Install. Amazon: under "Deliver to" choose your Fire TV, then tap Get. Use the same Google or Amazon account the TV is signed in to. Then come back to this page.

    TV not listed there? On the TV, open the app store, search for Downloader (orange icon, by AFTVnews) and install it. Either way, open it on the TV and choose Allow if it asks about files.

    In Downloader's box, type this address and press Go:

    {{APP_ADDRESS}}

    The download starts by itself.

  3. Press Install.

    The first time, the TV says it is not allowed to install unknown apps. Choose Settings, switch Downloader on, press Back, then Install again.

  4. Open Tally on the TV. It finds this server by itself and shows a 6-digit code. Type the code here:

    No code on the TV? Choose Add Server there and enter {{SERVER}}, then Add User.

Updates. When a new version is out, Tally opens an update screen by itself: press Download & Update, then Update, and open Tally again. The very first time, the TV asks once more to allow installs, this time for Tally: choose Settings, switch Tally on, press Back twice and press Download & Update again. You can delete Downloader afterwards; it is not needed again.

Samsung and LG TVs can't install apps from a phone, so Tally goes on from a computer on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. You do it once; after that, Tally updates itself from this server.

  1. On the computer, open the guide for your TV and follow it:

    Samsung guide2020 or newer; 2023 and newer need a free Samsung account LG guide2020 or newer; needs a free LG developer account

  2. When the installer asks for the server, type:

    {{SERVER}}

  3. Tally opens on the TV with a 6-digit code. On this phone, open Quick Connect, sign in if it asks, type the code and press Authorize.

  1. On the TV, install Swiftfin (Apple TV) or Jellyfin (Roku, Xbox, older Samsung and LG TVs) from its app store.

  2. Enter this server address and sign in:

    {{SERVER}}

  3. On your phone, open Live TV on this server, tap TV at the top and pick your TV.

    Now any game you tap on the phone starts playing on the TV.
